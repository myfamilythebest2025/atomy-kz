Atomy marketing plan, in plain language

In short: Atomy is a Korean online store with a membership system. You register for free and buy products for yourself at member prices.

And if you like the products and tell your friends about them, the company shares part of the money from their purchases with you. There are no mandatory purchases and no sales targets: you can simply keep buying for yourself.

1Points instead of percentages

Besides its price, every product has points (PV). Rewards are calculated from those points, so the amount is equally fair in every country.

Personal pointsyour own purchases
Group pointsyour team's purchases
Never expirethey just keep adding up
100%every purchase counts in full
2First step — 10,000 personal points

This is the only requirement to take part in payouts. You can reach personal PV gradually through your regular purchases; they never expire. Once you reach 10,000 PV, the condition is met for good.

3Two legs — left and right

Every member has just two places in the team: a left leg and a right one. Everyone who joins after that is placed below them — with no limit on depth.

People invited by your partners also land in your legs — the team keeps growing even without you.

4Payout — for a “step”

When both legs reach the required PV volume, a binary step is completed and the company pays a reward. PV in the legs can carry forward until the volume needed for a step is reached. At most one step per day is counted.

One more very important point: Sales Master is counted for one period — from the 1st to the 15th, or from the 16th to the end of the month. The official plan specifically identifies these two periods.

6Seven mastership levels

Mastership levels are counted separately: the required volume must be reached within one qualification period, not accumulated over years. The first level — Sales Master — is awarded when each leg reaches 2,500,000 PV during that period. In a new period, the mastership volume is counted from scratch; rewards also include gifts and trips from the company.

7Where this money comes from

Atomy has no middlemen, no shops and no expensive advertising — the company gives that budget to the people who spread the word about its products.
